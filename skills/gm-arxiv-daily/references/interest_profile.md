# Gabriele's arXiv interest profile (v2, 2026-11-23)

Reader: Gabriele Montefalcone, cosmologist / astroparticle theorist (UW-Madison). Built from 935
papers Gabriele saved between Dec 2023 and Sep 2026, their 23 INSPIRE papers, 37,422 papers
listed in astro-ph.CO + hep-ph over the same period, and two interview rounds.

Base rates: a mailing has ~47 new + cross-listed papers; Gabriele saves ~1.2 per day (7.6% of
astro-ph.CO primaries, 1.1% of hep-ph primaries, 0.8% of cross-lists). When asked to pick a top
list by hand they chose 2-5 papers per day, never padding.

## Tier 1 — top-list candidates

### 1a. Own research — a paper that builds on, tests, or competes with Gabriele's work
- **Warm inflation**: dissipation and the thermal bath during inflation, warm-inflation
  perturbations (WarmSPy), thermal gravitons, DM production during warm inflation, swampland
  constraints on inflation and dark energy.
- **Neutrino / light-relic phase shift** in CMB and 21-cm acoustic oscillations; free-streaming
  vs self-interacting neutrinos; N_eff and light relics for SO / CMB-S4 / CMB-HD.
- **Non-standard DM production**: freeze-in, low reheating temperature, WIMP-like DM without
  thermalization at freeze-out.
- **DM energy injection**: CMB (and 21-cm) limits on DM decay, annihilation and absorption
  (leptophilic DM, light vector / axial-vector DM).
- **Dark-energy phenomenology**: density-level parametrizations (f_DE), priors and Bayesian
  evidence for dynamical dark energy in DESI. This includes the steady stream (~1 per mailing) of
  papers fitting new w(z) / rho_DE(z) parametrizations or non-parametric reconstructions
  (w(z), Omega_m(z), H(z)) to DESI + SN + CC data: Gabriele saved 15% of them (5x their base
  rate), tracking them as competitors to their own work. Even when incremental, the best one or
  two of the day belong in the also list; a genuinely new idea belongs in the top list.

### 1b. Core interests (same priority as 1a when the paper has a substantive new result)
- DESI BAO / full-shape results, dynamical dark energy, phantom crossing, w0wa, optimal pivots;
  Hubble tension, early dark energy, recombination modifications.
- BBN, N_eff, light relics, cosmological neutrino-mass bounds, neutrino self-interactions.
- 21-cm, cosmic dawn, dark ages; reionization and the optical depth tau.
- CMB experiment results: ACT DR6, SPT-3G, Simons Observatory; lensing, birefringence, kSZ / tSZ,
  patchy screening, velocity reconstruction, cross-correlations.
- **Gravitational-wave cosmology**: dark / standard sirens, GW cosmography, H0 from GWs.

### Always in the top list
- Major collaboration data releases or key-result papers (DESI, ACT, SPT, SO, Planck legacy,
  Euclid key cosmology, LiteBIRD / CMB-S4 collaboration forecasts).

## Tier 2 — "also interesting" list
Listed roughly in priority order:
- **DM particle theory**: Sommerfeld enhancement, bound states, unitarity, portals, annihilation
  and decay channels; DM at colliders and direct detection (exception to the collider skip below
  when DM is the subject). A strong hep-ph DM theory paper may enter the top list.
- **Ultralight bosons**: black-hole superradiance, gravitational atoms, axion / ALP cosmology (CMB
  conversions, patchy screening, isocurvature, line-intensity probes). A strong one may enter
  the top list.
- DM-electron scattering, sub-GeV DM, direct-detection anomalies (e.g. the LZ 248 keV event).
- Primordial features, primordial power spectrum, non-Gaussianity.
- Gravitational waves: PTA, first-order phase transitions, primordial tensor modes.
- Inflation model-building that is not warm inflation (UV completions of Starobinsky,
  cosmological-collider signals, non-attractor / Lyth-bound papers). **Never the top list.**
- Primordial black holes, incl. memory burden (demoted by Gabriele from Tier 1).
- JWST high-z galaxies and early structure formation (demoted by Gabriele from Tier 1).
- Self-interacting DM, DM substructure.

## Tier 3 — only if unusually strong
- Inference tools, emulators, differentiable Boltzmann codes and generators, ML for cosmology.
- Galaxy simulations (DREAMS-type); distance-ladder H0 (Cepheids, TRGB, JAGB).

## Skip
- Collider, QCD, hadron / meson spectroscopy, lattice, PDFs, heavy-ion, flavour, jets, and
  neutrino-oscillation experiment phenomenology with no cosmology link — unless DM is the subject.

## Selection rules
1. **Top list: at most 5, never padded.** A paper enters only if it is Tier 1 (or a release, or
   an exceptional Tier-2 DM / ultralight-boson paper) *and* has a substantive new result. On a thin
   day show 2-3. Test: "would Gabriele open this PDF today?"
2. **Balance**: across the top list aim for about 3 cosmology : 2 particle phenomenology when
   candidates of comparable strength exist. Do not force it.
3. **Also list: up to 5**, from the remaining Tier 1 and Tier 2. It may exceed 5 only for papers
   with a direct connection to a Tier-1a topic (testing, extending, or competing with Gabriele's
   own work).
4. **One wildcard** at most, counted within the also list's 5: a paper outside the profile that is unusual or
   broadly striking (past picks: space-based particle colliders, the pulsar radial acceleration
   relation, a differentiable neutrino event generator). Tag it as a wildcard.
5. **Recent boost**: topics in `recent_boost.md` get a temporary bump within their tier;
   ignore rows whose Expires date has passed.
